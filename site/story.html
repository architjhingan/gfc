Our story

Tea has always been more than a drink.

It's the morning ritual. The quick catch-up that turns into an hour. The excuse to take five. The conversations that stay with you.

Tea has always had a way of bringing people together. We think it deserves a little more excitement.

Green Flag Club is our modern take on that ritual. Great tea, unexpected flavours and drinks you genuinely look forward to.
