Our matcha

Shade-grown, stone-ground, never rushed.

We buy one harvest a year, direct from a family farm in Uji, and we whisk to order. That's the whole method — the rest is just keeping our hands off it.

First harvest only

Ichibancha leaves picked in early May, shaded for three weeks before cutting. Sweeter, deeper green, zero chalk.

Ground on granite

Traditional stone mills turn slow enough to keep the leaf cool, so the aroma survives the trip to your cup.

Whisked, not blitzed

Bamboo chasen, 80°C water, W-motion. It takes ninety seconds longer and it tastes like a different drink.
