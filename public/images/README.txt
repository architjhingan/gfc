The four drink shots are all cropped 4:5 with the cup centred and filling the
same share of frame, which is what makes the grids look like one set. No filter
is applied, so they stay true to the originals.

Full-resolution originals live in brand/photography/, named to match.

Regenerating a drink shot from its original:
  sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 72 -Z 1250 \
    brand/photography/oat-matcha-latte.png --out public/images/oat-matcha-latte.jpg
